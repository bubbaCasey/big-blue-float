About the float

Big Blue is an ocean profiling float adopted by Swampscott High School through the Adopt a Float program (GO-BGC project). It was launched in October 2026, named after Swampscott High School, and is an autonomous float that drifts with ocean currents while it measures temperature, salinity, oxygen, nitrate, chlorophyll, and pH with depth.

The float's data is used in AP Environmental Science, Meteorology, and marine science classes. Students use this site to look at how the ocean's temperature and chemistry change with depth and over time.

Where the data comes from

Profiles come from the MBARI float data file for WMO 1902753: www3.mbari.org/lobo/Data/FloatVizData/1902753.txt. A script converts that file into data/float.json. The tracking and data pages read that file, and it is refreshed automatically every 6 hours.

Quality flags follow MBARI's convention: 0 = good, 4 = questionable, 8 = bad, 1 = missing or not inspected. Values flagged bad or not inspected are left out of this site. Values marked questionable are shown, so students should treat them with some care. Missing values are shown as gaps.

Positions for some profiles are interpolated when the float's own fix was missing. These are marked with quality flag 4 in MBARI's file.
